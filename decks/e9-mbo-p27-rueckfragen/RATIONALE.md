# Rationale for the deck e9-mbo-p27-rueckfragen (follow-up questions on § 27 MBO)

As of 17 Nov 2026, deck version 0.1.0. Method: `docs/QUESTION_DESIGN.md`. Public, so no expected verdicts.

## Purpose
A follow-up deck for **one** expert. He reviewed the OEKS formalisation of § 27 MBO (load-bearing walls) for faithfulness to its sources. His remarks went into a revised version, which was used to generate and test new checking rules. In doing so, five cases and four questions came up that need a judgement from practice:
- **Cases K1–K5:** reliefs in § 27 Abs. 1 Satz 3, namely storeys in the roof space (Nr. 1) and balconies or open walkways (Nr. 2). The rule tests showed that a published expert rule does not take these reliefs into account. Here the expert checks how they are to be read.
- **R1:** "§ 29 Abs. 4 bleibt unberührt": is it an exception to the relief, or a provision that applies alongside it? The expert's review left this open. In the revised formalisation it is recorded as an open question.
- **R2:** mapping the fire-resistance classes to the European classes (R / EI). It comes from a remark in his review and is to be recorded as a binding hint.
- **R3, R4:** two statements that were restructured because of his remarks, and that he has not seen in the new form. Confirming them raises their maturity to "expert-reviewed".
- **R5:** consent to use his answers in a publication, and whether to name him.

## Design decisions
- **Cases before questions:** the cases come first so that the judgement is made on the concrete case before R1 and R3 present our own summary.
- **Order mixed:** roof storey and balcony alternate, and the case pairs are not shown next to each other.
- **Neutral:** the options in R1 to R4 all have a one-line consequence of the same form, and "Hängt ab" is explicitly welcome.
- **No figure:** the existing duplex plan shows neither a roof space nor a balcony. The cases are described in words.

## Known weaknesses
- **"möglich" versus "eingerichtet" (K3):** the case deliberately says that Aufenthaltsräume would be possible but none is set up. This is the reading point; "Hängt ab" with a reason is the expected answer type.
- **Balcony wall panel (K2, K4):** whether a wall panel that only borders the balcony counts as part of the balcony is itself a matter of interpretation.
- **R3 shows our summary:** it can anchor an answer to R1, which is why R1 is asked before it.
